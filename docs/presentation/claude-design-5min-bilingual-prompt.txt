THE CENTRAL STORY

Keep this central line explicit:
“Lethe turns fragments of a life into a living garden.”
“Lethe 把人生的片段，变成一座有生命的花园。”

A memory becomes a flower. Shared people, places, and feelings create threads.
Care can reach connected memories. With limited light, the visitor chooses what
to tend. A season changes the garden. Some flowers fade into outlines or seeds.
Original words remain, and returning is a deliberate action. The resulting
garden expresses the relationships and attention that shape this life.

Use this emotional progression to guide pacing:
recognition, attachment, choice, visible consequence, quiet, reflection.

Our fictional episode begins with 23 authored memories, one remaining light,
and one optional planting that brings it to 24. The playable episode pauses
between actions and advances 21 fictional garden days when requested. Do not
say three real weeks elapse during the stage demonstration. Personal gardens
have separate real-time rules. Do not explain the mathematical formulas during
the main talk.
